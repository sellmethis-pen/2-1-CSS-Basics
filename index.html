Ferret

The ferret is awesome because its like a fluffy snake with legs. I like snakes, but everything is better fluffier, so ferrets are the almighty solution. I do wish ferrets didn't have legs, so they could slither around. Ferrets are natural predators with huge egos, so they will try attacking anything regardless of size, whether it's me, my 80 pound pitbull-labrador, or my kitten (no animals have been harmed). The ferret can dance, steal, and hide very well— some things lots of humans can't do properly. I cannot forget to mention their rancid stench that reminds me of a certain breed of persons called the Discord moderator. These animals are to live for and they are whelming.

Jokes aside, they are my favorite animal. I love ferrets and I have 3; Butters, Marjorine, and Abigail. Butters and Marjorine are named after South Park characters and Abigail is named after a Stardew Valley character.
